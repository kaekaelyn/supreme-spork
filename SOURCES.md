# Sources

Every scientific claim in the design documents traces to an entry here. `source:` keys in [`data/species.yaml`](data/species.yaml) resolve against this file.

**Verification status** is tracked honestly, because the whole project rests on it:

- ✅ **Verified** — located and its content confirmed during research for these documents.
- ⚠️ **Needs verification** — drawn from background knowledge and consistent with everything found, but the primary source was not read directly. **Confirm before any of this reaches the published dataset.**

## Derived, not cited

Claims computed or reasoned from first principles rather than taken from a paper. Each should be sanity-checked by an advisor.

| Claim | Basis |
|---|---|
| **Day length ~23.4 h at 125 Ma** | Tidal deceleration of ~1.7 ms/century over 125 Myr ≈ 0.59 h shorter than a modern day. Consistent with published length-of-day reconstructions but not taken from one |
| **Constellations unrecognisable** | Stellar proper motion over 125 Myr vastly exceeds the timescale on which constellations are stable (~10⁵–10⁶ yr) |
| **Moon ~0.3% closer** | Lunar recession of ~3.8 cm/yr over 125 Myr ≈ 4,750 km, against a 384,400 km mean distance |
| **Toxicology of cycad, ginkgo, and fern foods** | Well-established botanical and ethnobotanical fact (cycasin, ginkgotoxin/antivitamin B6, thiaminase) applied to the Yixian's attested plant groups. The *groups* are attested; the toxin chemistry is extrapolated from modern relatives |
| **Conifer needle infusion as an antiscorbutic** | Well-documented historical/ethnobotanical practice, applied to the formation's conifer-dominated flora |
| **Conifer bark cambium as famine food; bark bast cordage; horsetail as abrasive; conifer pitch glue** | Ethnographic practice from boreal cultures, applied to attested plant groups. Birch tar is explicitly *unavailable* — no Betulaceae this early |
| **Protein poisoning ("rabbit starvation")** | Established human nutritional physiology |
| **No honeybees / no honey** | Social corbiculate bees are an angiosperm-era radiation; the Yixian's angiosperm flora is ~5 species |
